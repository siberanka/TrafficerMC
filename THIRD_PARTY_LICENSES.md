# Third-party notices

TrafficerMC uses third-party packages installed from `package.json`. Their complete license texts are provided by the installed packages and must accompany redistributed bundles as required by each license.

Key runtime projects include:

- [Mineflayer](https://github.com/PrismarineJS/mineflayer) — MIT
- [node-minecraft-protocol](https://github.com/PrismarineJS/node-minecraft-protocol) — BSD-3-Clause
- [minecraft-data](https://github.com/PrismarineJS/minecraft-data) — MIT
- [prismarine-nbt](https://github.com/PrismarineJS/prismarine-nbt) — MIT
- [Electron](https://github.com/electron/electron) — MIT
- [Vite](https://github.com/vitejs/vite) — MIT

Paper, AuthMe Reloaded, ViaVersion, and proxy server software are test fixtures only. Their binaries are deliberately ignored and are not redistributed in this repository. Obtain them from their official projects and follow their respective licenses and usage terms.
